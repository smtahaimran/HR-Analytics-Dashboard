# HR Analytics Dashboard

A one-page Power BI dashboard analyzing employee attrition drivers, built to help HR leadership identify at-risk segments and prioritize retention interventions.

## Problem Statement:

I have to analyzes an HR dataset to understand the key factors driving employee attrition (16.4% annually) and to identify which workforce segments and conditions are most associated with turnover. The goal is to uncover actionable insights and recommend interventions that could help reduce attrition.

---



## Dataset:

- **1,470 employee records**
- **31 columns** covering demographics, compensation, tenure, satisfaction scores, and attrition status
- Fields include: Department, Job Role, OverTime, Job Satisfaction, Work-Life Balance, Monthly Income, Years at Company, and more

## Dashboard Overview:

The one-page dashboard includes:

**KPI Cards**
- Total Workforce
- Employees Lost
- Overall Attrition Rate
- Average Monthly Income

**Slicers**
- Department
- OverTime
- Age Group
- Gender

**Visuals**
1. Attrition Rate by OverTime
2. Attrition Rate by JobStatisfaction
3. Attrition Count by Department
4. Avg Monthly Income, Attrited vs Retained
5. Attrition Rate by Tenure Band

---

## Key Insights:

- Overtime staff leave **3.4x more often** (33.8% vs 9.8%)
- Low job satisfaction nearly **doubles** attrition (23.5% vs 12.0%)
- **New hires (0–2 years)** are the highest-risk group at 20.3%, dropping to 10.3% after 10+ years
- **R&D** has the highest attrition rate (17.1%) and the most total exits
- Employees who left earned less on average (**$6.2K vs $6.7K**) — pay is a secondary driver

---

## Recommendations:

- Reduce overtime burden in R&D and Sales
- Provide better facilities and support to enhance job satisfaction
- Strengthen onboarding and check-ins for employees in their first 2 years
- Review and adjust pay for at-risk roles to reduce attrition
- Investigate R&D-specific attrition drivers given its elevated rate
